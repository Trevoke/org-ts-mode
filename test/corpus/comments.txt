Multiple comments
==================

# First comment

==================
Comment under headline
==================

* Headline
# This is a comment under the headline

---

(document
  (headline
    (stars)
    (title))
  (comment))

==================
Comments between elements
==================

* First headline
# Comment
* Second headline

---

(document
  (headline
    (stars)
    (title))
  (comment)
  (headline
    (stars)
    (title)))
