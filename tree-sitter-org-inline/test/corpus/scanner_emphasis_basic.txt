==================
Multiple emphasis together
==================

This *bold* and /italic/ and _underline_ work.

---

(inline
  (title_only
    (title
      (plain_text)
      (text_markup
        (bold
          (plain_text)))
      (plain_text)
      (text_markup
        (italic
          (plain_text)))
      (plain_text)
      (text_markup
        (underline
          (plain_text)))
      (plain_text))))
