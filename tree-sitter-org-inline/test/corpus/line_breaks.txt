==================
Known limitation: Backslash before line break not detected
==================

text\\\\
more

---

; KNOWN LIMITATION: Scanner can't see grammar-consumed chars (ERROR).
; The first \\ becomes ERROR, then the second \\ matches line_break
; because last_char is stale. Same root cause as emphasis PRE issue.
(inline
  (title_only
    (title
      (plain_text)
      (ERROR)
      (line_break)
      (plain_text))))

==================
Single backslash at EOL becomes entity (not line break)
==================

text\
more

---

; The \ at EOL combines with "more" on next line to form entity
; because newline is an extra (skipped between tokens)
(inline
  (title_only
    (title
      (plain_text)
      (entity))))

==================
Invalid: Double backslash not at end of line (ERROR + entity, not line_break)
