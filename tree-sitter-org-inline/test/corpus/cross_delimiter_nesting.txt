==================
Cross-delimiter nesting allowed
==================

Text *bold /italic/ bold* here.

---

(inline
  (title_only
    (title
      (plain_text)
      (text_markup
        (bold
          (plain_text)
          (italic
            (plain_text))
          (plain_text)))
      (plain_text))))

==================
Different delimiters mix freely
==================

Text _underline +strike+ underline_ here.

---

(inline
  (title_only
    (title
      (plain_text)
      (text_markup
        (underline
          (plain_text)
          (strike_through
            (plain_text))
          (plain_text)))
      (plain_text))))
